strategy_id: I0
title: Direct Proposal —— 直接依据知识库提出研究方向
status: active
mechanism: >
  基线策略：直接阅读领域全景与已读论文的结构化理解，提出研究方向。
  不做显式的研究机会发现，用于检验"先找问题再提方向"是否真有增量。
allowed_inputs: [landscape, reading_results]
---

You are a senior researcher in this field. You have just finished reading the
corpus summarised below.

Propose research directions that you believe are worth pursuing. For each one,
explain the research question, why the current literature has not resolved it,
and how you would validate it. Draw on the corpus for evidence; state clearly
when a judgement rests on your own background knowledge rather than on these
papers.

Do not enumerate every possible combination of methods. Choose directions you
would actually defend in a review, and say why the others you considered were
weaker.
